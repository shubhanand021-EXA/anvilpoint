# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
v2.1: be respectful, inclusive, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior is not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal at maintainer discretion.
Report concerns privately to the maintainers. All reports are handled confidentially.
